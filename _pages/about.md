I am a Tenure-Track Associate Professor at [Shanghai Center for Mathematical Sciences (SCMS), Fudan University](https://scms.fudan.edu.cn/). I joined SCMS in September 2022 as a Young Investigator and became a Tenure-Track Associate Professor in September 2025.

Before joining SCMS, I was a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor in the [Department of Statistics at Texas A&M University](https://stat.tamu.edu/) (09/2019--08/2022). 

My research interests include random networks, extreme value theory, network inference, applied probability and stochastic processes, and mathematical statistics.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research from the [School of Operations Research and Information Engineering at Cornell University](https://www.orie.cornell.edu) in August 2019, under the supervision of Prof. Sidney Resnick.
